# Microphone Tips for Clear Voice Recognition

To get the highest accuracy and cleanest voice recognition with Gnani Prisma and VoiceQueue, follow these best practices:

1. **Use Earphones with a Microphone**
   - Headsets or wired earphones with an inline microphone isolate your voice from room echo and background reverberation much better than built-in laptop or desktop microphones.

2. **Keep the Microphone 15 to 20 cm from Your Mouth**
   - Position the microphone capsule approximately 15 to 20 cm (6 to 8 inches) away from your lips.
   - Do not hold the microphone directly against your mouth, as breathing and plosive puffs ("p", "t", "b") cause clipping and distortion.

3. **Use a Quiet Room**
   - Minimize ambient noise: turn off or step away from loud ceiling fans, air conditioners, street windows, televisions, and loud chatter.

4. **Speak One Short, Natural Sentence**
   - Clearly state your request in one complete sentence (e.g., *"Naaku repu token kavali"* or *"I need a token for tomorrow"*).
   - Avoid long pauses mid-sentence or conversational rambling.

5. **Release the Key Right After Finishing**
   - Press and hold the **SPACE** bar (or tap the microphone button) before you begin speaking.
   - Release the **SPACE** bar (or tap to stop) immediately after your last syllable to prevent recording unnecessary silence or room echo.
